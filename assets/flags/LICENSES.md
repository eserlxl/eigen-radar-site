# Flag provenance

National flags, taken from Wikimedia Commons. A flag's design is a state
symbol rather than a copyrighted work in most jurisdictions, and the SVG
renderings on Commons are published under free licences; the per-file terms
are on each file's Commons page.

Resolved from Wikidata's flag-image property (P41) for each ISO 3166-1
alpha-2 code, preferring the canonical `Flag of <name>.svg` title.
Refresh: `python3 scripts/fetch-flags.py --write`
